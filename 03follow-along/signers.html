Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

Thomas Jefferson
Thomas Jefferson, a delegate from Virginia, was the principal author of the Declaration of Independence. He later served as the 3rd President of the United States and is remembered for his contributions to American ideals of liberty and democracy.
John Adams
John Adams, representing Massachusetts, was a vocal advocate for independence in the Continental Congress. He became the 2nd President of the United States and played a key role in drafting foundational documents and promoting the cause of freedom.
Benjamin Franklin
Benjamin Franklin, representing Pennsylvania, was a respected scientist, inventor, writer, and diplomat. He helped shape the Declaration of Independence and later helped gain French support for the American Revolution.
John Hancock
John Hancock, representing Massachusetts, was President of the Continental Congress. He is especially remembered for his large and distinctive signature on the Declaration of Independence.
Samuel Adams
Samuel Adams, representing Massachusetts, was a strong supporter of American independence and helped organize resistance against British rule.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
Charles Carroll of Carrollton, representing Maryland, supported American independence and was the only Catholic signer of the Declaration of Independence. He was also the last surviving signer.

Each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the British Crown.
